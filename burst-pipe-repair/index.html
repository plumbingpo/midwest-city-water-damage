A burst pipe is one of the most common water damage events a property can face, and potentially one of the most destructive. One broken line can put out 4 to 8 gallons every minute, so 240 to 480 gallons an hour may be running through floors, ceilings and walls. Pipes burst from freezing, old plumbing, too much water pressure, corrosion or a physical hit, and in every case a fast emergency response is what keeps water from ruining flooring, structural materials, building systems and your belongings.

Midwest City Restoration provides 24/7 emergency response for burst pipe water damage throughout Midwest City and surrounding areas. We can help find the shutoff and stop the water, and our team works with licensed plumbers to see that the pipe itself is repaired correctly. Once the water source is stopped, we immediately begin extraction with truck-mounted and portable equipment, removing standing water from all affected areas as quickly as possible.

Following extraction, our structural drying process addresses all moisture-affected materials, including drywall, insulation, wood framing, subflooring and finished flooring. Since water runs downhill through ceiling spaces, floor assemblies and wall cavities, a burst pipe frequently affects several rooms on more than one level. Our thermal imaging and moisture detection equipment traces water migration paths so every affected area is identified and properly dried, which prevents the mold growth and structural deterioration that hidden moisture causes.

Why Burst Pipe Repair Is Not a DIY Job

What you can see after a pipe bursts is usually only part of the damage. It moves between ceiling joists, inside floor assemblies, beside electrical conduit and down wall cavities, soaking materials you might not find for days or even weeks. By then, mold may already be established in hidden spaces and structural materials may be significantly weakened. With moisture meters and thermal cameras, trained technicians follow the water's path and pinpoint every affected area right away.

Professional-grade extraction and drying equipment also works on a completely different scale than consumer tools. Compared with a shop vacuum, truck-mounted extraction is hundreds of times faster, and compared with a residential dehumidifier, a commercial one removes 10 to 20 times as much moisture. With only 24 to 48 hours before mold starts, you need equipment this fast and this strong.

Maintenance Tips for Burst Pipe Repair

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • If your home still has polybutylene or aging galvanized steel lines, replace them before one gives out
  • Find the main shutoff valve now and practice closing it so you can do it fast
  • Install water leak detection sensors near water heaters and main supply lines
  • Pressure higher than 80 PSI wears on pipes and their fittings, so test yours from time to time
  • Unheated spaces like garages, attics and crawl spaces need pipe insulation on exposed lines
  • Disconnect garden hoses and shut off outdoor faucets before the first freeze

Signs It Is Time for Burst Pipe Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • In a cold snap, exposed lines that look frosted or frozen over
  • A hidden leak often shows up first as a much larger water bill
  • No water at a tap in freezing weather, a sign the line is frozen
  • Faucets and showers across the building suddenly running weak
  • Exposed pipes that drip, mist or shoot water
  • Puddles on the floor that you cannot trace to anything obvious
  • Damp, moldy odors around sinks, tubs or walls that hide pipes
