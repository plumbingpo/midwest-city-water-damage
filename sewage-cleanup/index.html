Of all the problems an owner can have, sewage coming up through the drains ranks among the most dangerous. Raw sewage contains dangerous bacteria, viruses, parasites and other pathogens, including E. coli, hepatitis, salmonella and giardia, that pose serious health risks to anyone who touches contaminated materials. Midwest City Restoration provides emergency sewage cleanup throughout Midwest City, responding 24/7 with properly trained technicians, appropriate personal protective equipment and EPA-registered disinfectants to decontaminate your property safely.

The industry classifies sewage as black water, or Category 3, which is the most dangerous and most contaminated type of water damage there is. That rating calls for the strictest protocols: containment barriers go up, crews wear full protective equipment, every porous material the sewage touched is removed, and the surfaces that stay are disinfected thoroughly. Our technicians follow IICRC S500 standards for Category 3 water damage so your property is restored to safe occupancy standards.

We clear the standing sewage first, then pull contaminated water out of structural materials, remove carpet, padding, insulation, drywall and any other porous item that was in contact with sewage for proper disposal, disinfect each hard surface, treat with antimicrobial products and finish with structural drying, which prevents secondary mold. To finish, thermal foggers and hydroxyl generators of industrial grade deodorize the space so the sewage smell does not linger.

Why Sewage Cleanup Is Not a DIY Job

No homeowner should try to clean up sewage personally. Handling raw sewage safely takes specialized training, EPA-registered disinfectants that consumers cannot buy, and personal protective equipment, meaning respirators, Tyvek suits and protective boots and gloves. People exposed to the pathogens in sewage can become seriously ill with skin and respiratory infections, hepatitis or gastrointestinal disease.

Professional sewage cleanup also requires proper disposal of contaminated materials as biohazard waste. Simply placing sewage-contaminated drywall, carpet and insulation in your regular trash is both illegal and hazardous to sanitation workers and the community. Certified restoration companies maintain proper waste disposal protocols and relationships with licensed biohazard waste disposal facilities.

How to Prevent Sewage Cleanup Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Address slow drains promptly, before they become complete blockages
  • Schedule a camera inspection of the sewer line every 2 to 3 years so trouble is found early
  • In an emergency a plumber needs the cleanout, so learn where yours is and keep it in good shape
  • Use drain strainers in showers and sinks to catch hair and debris
  • Wipes clog sewer lines even when the label says flushable, so only toilet paper goes down the toilet
  • Keep downspouts and sump pump discharge out of the sanitary sewer so it does not overload in heavy rain

Signs You Need Sewage Cleanup in Midwest City

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Toilet water with waste in it soaking into carpet or running across the floor
  • Dark residue from sewage left on the basement floor or ringing a drain
  • Toilets or drains that gurgle whenever you run water somewhere else
  • Damp patches on a wall or floor that also smell foul
  • Sewage or dark water backing up through floor drains, toilets or tubs
  • Every sink, tub and shower draining slowly even after you plunge them
  • More than one drain in the house overflowing or backing up together
